How it works

1. Parse

Name, contacts, education, experience and skills are pulled out of your file.

2. Rank

TF-IDF and cosine similarity compare your resume with the job post, along with skill coverage.

3. Prepare

Missing skills turn into topics and practice questions for your interview.

FAQ

Is this AI?

It uses NLP techniques (text parsing, TF-IDF, cosine similarity) and hand-written scoring rules. No large language model is involved.

Is my resume stored?

No. The server reads the file in memory and sends back the report.

Does a high score guarantee a job?

No. It is a guide for improving your resume, not a hiring decision.
